# A place to think

Good work rarely begins with a finished idea. It begins with a question, a few observations, and enough room to connect them.

This notebook follows a small project: turning scattered reading notes into a clear essay. Keep the workspace familiar, the structure simple, and the next step visible.

## From notes to a draft

Collect what matters. Write down why it matters. When two observations point in the same direction, give that connection a name.

### Working principles

- **Read with a question.** A useful note records more than a quotation.
- **Make connections explicit.** Explain how one idea changes another.
- **Leave room to revise.** An outline is a starting point, not a contract.
